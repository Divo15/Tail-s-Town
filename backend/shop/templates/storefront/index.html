Smart care, clearly answered.

Quick guidance for choosing and using the Tail's Town products that fit your pet's routine.

What makes Tail's Town smart pet-care products different?

Each product pairs useful everyday automation with a clean, home-friendly design. The goal is straightforward: less routine work for you and dependable care for your pets.

Which products are available in the Tail's Town collection?

The core collection includes the Smart Feeder, Water Fountain, and Litter Box. You can explore each product separately or browse care bundles for a more complete routine.

Is the Smart Feeder suitable for both cats and dogs?

Yes. The Smart Feeder is designed for consistent scheduled portions and can support everyday feeding routines for cats and dogs.

How does the Water Fountain support daily hydration?

The fountain keeps water moving through a filtered flow, with a low-splash edge and a visible water level that make daily hydration easier to monitor.

Is the Litter Box designed for indoor homes?

Yes. Its rounded enclosed form creates a more private litter corner while keeping the product visually calm enough for shared indoor spaces.

Can I buy Tail's Town products as a bundle?

Yes. Tail's Town offers focused cat-care and dog-care bundles that combine the products used most often in each daily routine.
