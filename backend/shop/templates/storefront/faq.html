Getting started

How do I set up a Tail's Town product?

Place the product on a stable, easy-to-reach surface, remove all packaging, and follow the setup steps included with your product. Give your pet a little time to investigate it before making it part of the daily routine.

Can I use Tail's Town products without the app?

Core everyday functions are designed to remain simple at the product itself. App features and connected controls depend on the specific product, so check its setup guide for the functions available to you.

02

Smart Feeder

Why is the feeder dispensing a different amount?

Food shape, size, and moisture can change how portions move through the hopper. Keep the hopper and bowl seated correctly, use food suited to the feeder opening, and compare a few dispenses before adjusting the schedule.

What should I do if food gets stuck?

Pause the feeder, empty the bowl, and check the food path for clumps or foreign objects. Clean and dry the removable parts before trying again. Never force the motor while the feeder is powered.

How can I help my pet accept the feeder?

Start with the feeder beside the usual bowl and keep the first portions small and familiar. Once your pet is comfortable with its sound and shape, gradually move it into the place where you want the daily routine to happen.

03

Water Fountain

How often should I clean the fountain?

Refresh the water regularly and clean the bowl and pump whenever you see buildup, hair, or a change in flow. Rinse the filter and replace it according to the product guide or sooner if it looks blocked.

Why has the fountain become noisy or stopped flowing?

Low water, trapped air, hair, or buildup around the pump can affect the sound and flow. Unplug the fountain, top it up, clean the pump area, and reseat the filter before testing it again.

04

Litter Box

How do I introduce my cat to the Litter Box?

Place it near the current litter area and use the same familiar litter at first. Keep the space calm, leave the entrance clear, and move the box gradually only after your cat is using it comfortably.

What is the best way to keep it fresh?

Remove waste on a regular schedule, wipe the areas your cat touches, and follow the product guide for deeper cleaning. Let every washable part dry fully before putting the box back into use.

Orders & care

Where can I find order, shipping, and return information?

Those details will live in the Support links in the footer as each page is added. Keep your order confirmation handy so our team can help you quickly with a delivery or return question.

What if these steps do not solve the problem?

Stop using the product if it is damaged or behaves unexpectedly. Note the product, the issue, and what you have already tried, then contact Tail's Town support with your order details for the next step.
