Where we begin

Small routines carry a lot of love.

Tail's Town started with a simple observation: the moments that keep pets well are often the ones repeated every day. Meals, fresh water, and a clean corner do not need to feel like chores that compete with the rest of life.

We make smart pet-care tools that are easy to understand, gentle in the home, and dependable when your day gets full.

Care, connected

Less checking. More being there.

Our feeder, fountain, and litter box are designed to fit into real homes and real schedules. They help make the essentials more consistent while leaving the important part untouched: the time you spend with your pet.
