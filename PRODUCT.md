# Product

## Register

product

## Users

PETKIT has two primary user groups: customers shopping for smart pet-care products, and staff managing products, carts, checkout orders, and customer support from the backend.

## Product Purpose

The product exists to turn the PETKIT storefront into a working ecommerce flow: browse products, add them to cart, place an order, and let staff manage products and fulfill orders from a clear admin area.

## Brand Personality

Clean, capable, and calm. Customer-facing pages should feel like smart pet-care retail. Admin pages should feel operational, trustworthy, and easy to scan.

## Anti-references

Avoid over-decorated admin screens, marketing-style layouts inside staff workflows, unclear tables, hidden customer contact details, and UI patterns that make order fulfillment slower.

## Design Principles

- Put the next operational action within reach.
- Prefer scannable data over decorative presentation in admin surfaces.
- Preserve the clean PETKIT retail tone without making staff tools feel like a landing page.
- Show order, contact, and fulfillment details together so staff do not hunt across screens.
- Keep customer-facing and staff-facing experiences visually related but functionally distinct.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-accessible controls, readable table layouts, and reduced reliance on color alone for status.
